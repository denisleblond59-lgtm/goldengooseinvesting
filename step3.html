set up your automatic investment

You've choosen your monthly amount. Now let's put your investing on autopilot. In a few minutes, you'll have a recurring monthly investment in VUAA.

1. Open Invest

Open the Invest tab in Revolut.

2. Search for VUAA

Search for "VUAA" (Vanguard S&P 500).

3. Tap Buy

Tap the Buy button on the VUAA screen.

4. Set up recurring

Choose recurring buy and enter your monthly amount.

ℹ️

Your Revolut screen may look slightly different depending on your country or app version, but the steps are always very similar.

🎉
Great! Your automatic investment is now active.

Your money will now work for you automatically every single month.
