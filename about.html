About Stefan & Golden Goose

Why I created this 4-step framework to make financial freedom accessible, simple, and automated for everyone.

Hi, I'm Stefan Reinders

Founder of Golden Goose & Financial Literacy Advocate

1. The Why: True Growth is About Sharing Freedom

For me, real growth in life isn't about accumulating more possessions or wealth. It's about gaining peace of mind, continuing to learn, and sharing what truly enriches our human experience.

I spent over 20 years working as an engineer and 30 years investing in the stock market. By saving disciplinedly, I was able to retire at 40 and gain full control over my time. But as my personal journey evolved, I realized that true fulfillment comes from helping others experience that same freedom.

Looking back, I wish someone had stripped away the financial jargon decades ago to show me just how simple building security can be. I wanted to pass that knowledge forward, and I desired to give people the clear, honest roadmap I wish I had back then. That is how I came to create Golden Goose.

2. The How: Stripping Away the Complexity

Building long-term wealth shouldn't require a finance degree, endless market monitoring, or paying high fees to intermediaries.

The strategy relies on a timeless principle: invest regularly in broad, low-cost index funds and let time do the work. I condensed everything I learned into a straightforward framework you can complete in 4 steps and about 30 minutes:

  • Minimal Costs: Built around institutional-grade, low-fee index funds like Vanguard's VUAA (0.07% annual management fee).
  • Automated Peace of Mind: Set up recurring investments with zero commission fees using accessible platforms like Revolut.
  • Complete Independence: No financial advisors taking a cut, no complicated contracts, and zero hidden mechanisms.

3. What’s in it for You: Freedom of Choice

This is not about a financial product. It is about your life, your time, and your autonomy.

It is about having the option to take a sabbatical, work on your own terms, retire earlier, or simply sleep better knowing your future is protected.

  • 100% Free & Transparent: I receive no commissions from banks or fund managers, I accept no user money, and I sell nothing.
  • Total Control: Your money remains entirely in your hands, allowing you to pause, adjust, or access your investments whenever you decide.

By taking 30 minutes today to automate your financial foundation, you give your future self the greatest luxury possible: choices.
