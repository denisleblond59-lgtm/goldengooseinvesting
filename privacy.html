Privacy Policy (GDPR)

Golden Goose respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

We do not collect personal data when you browse our site or use our financial calculators. Your calculation parameters remain locally within your browser session. Golden Goose operates with minimal technical cookies required for website performance. We do not sell user data to third parties.

3. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction, or deletion of any personal communications sent to us.
